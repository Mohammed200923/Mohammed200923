Live with real NASA data Online

mohammed200923-skyshift.hf.space. All five challenge targets were checked on this link on 14 November.

Try it above

  • Press play. Barnard's Star moves north along the cyan predicted track.
  • Tick Difference vs. median sky. Still stars fade out, and anything that moved becomes a blue/orange pair.
  • Click the moving star. Its spectrum and light curve appear on the right.
  • Set Show → Every exposure and scrub to visit 3 to catch the asteroid crossing the field.
  • Try the other test targets with the chips under the search box.

Where the real data comes from

  • NASA SPHEREx Level-2 images from IRSA (SIA v2 search plus cutout service). This is the core of the tool.
  • ESA Gaia DR3 via CDS VizieR, for star motions.
  • CDS Sesame / SIMBAD to look up object names.
  • 2MASS, AllWISE, DSS2 background maps through Aladin Lite.

Day-1 checks on the live archive

  • IRSA search returns rows for collection spherex_qr, or we find the current name.
  • The rows contain access_url, t_min and em_min/em_max.
  • The cutout link ?center=RA,DEC&size=0.1 returns the IMAGE, FLAGS and ZODI planes.
  • Barnard's Star shifts about 1 pixel per visit. WISE 0855 appears only at 4–5 µm.

SkyShift

Type any position in the sky and watch NASA's SPHEREx images play as a time-lapse, with WISE and NEOWISE going back to 2010. Anything that moves or changes brightness stands out, and is checked against Gaia DR3, CatWISE2020 and JPL's list of known asteroids and comets.

3D sky

Fly to a target on an all-sky globe and step through its observations. Best place to start.

Interface: Ibrahim

Explorer

Browse targets, compare two dates side by side, and read each image's details. English and Arabic.

Interface: Ibrahim

Analysis

The full engine: any RA/Dec, the 102 SPHEREx bands, automatic moving-object search, light curves, overlays, export.

Engine: SkyShift

Challenge targets in the analysis view: Barnard's Star · Epsilon Indi B · WISE 0855−0714 · RW Aur · R Dra

Data: NASA/IPAC IRSA (SPHEREx, AllWISE, NEOWISE, CatWISE2020), Gaia DR3 at MAST, JPL Small-Body Identification and Horizons, CSA NEOSSat at CADC. All three views run on the same live data. First visit to a new field takes a few minutes; after that it is cached.

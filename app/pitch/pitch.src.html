How it works

Four steps, done automatically in seconds

STEP 1

Fetch the photos

Every SPHEREx photo of that spot, from NASA’s archive.

STEP 2

Clean them up

Remove the glow of space dust and faulty pixels.

STEP 3

Line them up

Every photo matched so still stars sit exactly on top.

STEP 4

Find and check

Spot what moves; confirm it in NASA photos back to 2010.

Can we trust it? Our maths matches the software professional astronomers use, checked by 20 automated tests.
